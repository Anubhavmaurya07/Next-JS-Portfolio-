Questions people ask

Does a root canal hurt?

Not during the treatment — the tooth is fully numbed first. You may feel some soreness for a day or two afterwards, which a regular painkiller handles.

Can I come without an appointment?

Yes, for pain or an emergency we'll see you the same day. For anything else, booking a slot means you won't wait.

Do you treat children?

Yes, from age 2. We keep their first visit short and fun, and the first check-up for children under 12 is free.

Can I claim the cost on insurance?

We give itemised bills and treatment notes that most insurers accept for reimbursement. Check your policy for dental cover.

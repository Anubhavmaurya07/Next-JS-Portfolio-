    House rules

    1. No combing hair at the table.
    2. No arguing about cricket after 10 pm.
    3. One bun maska is never enough. Order two.
    4. Do not ask for less sugar. The chai knows best.
    5. Laptops welcome before 11 am, not at lunch.
    6. Pay at the counter. Bargaining with the owner is not allowed.

    By order, the management

    Three generations, one chai

    Rustom Irani opened the café in 1958 and named it after his wife, who baked the first mawa cakes in the back kitchen.

    His grandchildren run it today. The bentwood chairs, the marble tables and the chai recipe haven't changed. The berry pulao arrived in 1972, and nobody has been allowed to touch that recipe either.
